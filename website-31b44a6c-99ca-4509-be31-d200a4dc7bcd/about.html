About VISDA Hospitals

Advanced technology. Expert care. Better tomorrow.

Who We Are

A dedicated orthopaedic centre in the heart of Kerala

VISDA Max Care Hospital — a unit of Divine Medical Centre Ltd — was established to bring advanced joint replacement surgery within reach of families in Thrissur, Palakkad and across central Kerala.

For years, patients needing complex knee or hip surgery were advised to travel to metro hospitals, adding cost, delay and the strain of being far from family during recovery. Our aim is simple: deliver that same standard of surgical technology and rehabilitation locally, in Wadakanchery.

We are proud to be among the first centres in Kerala and South India to offer minimally invasive robotic knee replacement.

Our Commitment

Precision. Every joint replacement is planned in three dimensions before surgery, then carried out with robotic guidance.

Honesty. Surgery is not always the answer. If your condition can be managed without an operation, we will tell you so.

Continuity. The same team that plans your surgery guides your physiotherapy and follow-up.
