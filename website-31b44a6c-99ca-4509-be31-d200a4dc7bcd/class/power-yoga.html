Intermediate–Advanced

Power Yoga

Strength, sweat, and serious results

60 min

Duration

Intermediate–Advanced

Level

Tanya, Tomi, Vera

Instructors

Power Yoga is an athletic, fitness-based approach that demands strength and stamina. Expect challenging sequences, a higher heart rate, and a deeply satisfying workout that still honours the mind-body connection.

✓ Builds full-body strength · ✓ Burns calories effectively · ✓ Boosts mental resilience · ✓ Improves balance and core stability

What to bring

Yoga mat, two water bottles, a small towel. Clothes that can handle some sweat.
