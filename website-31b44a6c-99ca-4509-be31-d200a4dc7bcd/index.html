About VISDA Hospitals

Advanced orthopaedic care, close to home

VISDA Max Care Hospital — a unit of Divine Medical Centre Ltd — is a dedicated orthopaedic and joint replacement centre in Wadakanchery, Kerala, led by Dr. Premkumar V. Our team combines robotic surgical technology with experienced surgeons and structured rehabilitation, so patients no longer need to travel to metro cities for advanced joint care.

  • Robotic-assisted knee and hip replacement
  • Modern modular operation theatres
  • In-house physiotherapy and rehabilitation
  • Personalised pre- and post-operative care plans
More About Us

Advanced Technology. Expert Care. Better Tomorrow.

Robotic assistance allows your surgeon to plan the procedure in three dimensions before the first incision, and to execute that plan with a level of consistency that is difficult to achieve by hand alone.

The result is a knee that feels more natural, with less soft-tissue disturbance and a recovery pathway that gets most patients walking on the same day.
