FAQ

Before You Roll Out The Mat…

Because every great journey starts with a few questions.

 What should I bring to my first class?

Just bring a yoga mat, comfortable clothes you can move in, and a water bottle. We provide props like blocks and straps in the studio.

Are memberships available?

Yes! We offer flexible class packs and monthly memberships to suit your schedule and commitment level.

How early should I arrive before class?

Arriving 10–15 minutes early gives you time to settle in, meet the instructor, and get comfortable.

Is there parking available near the studio?

Yes! We have free parking right outside the studio for all our students.

Can I try a class before joining?

Definitely. We offer a drop-in option and intro passes so you can try before you commit.
