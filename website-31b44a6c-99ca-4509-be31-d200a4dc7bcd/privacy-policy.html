Privacy Policy

Note for the site owner: this is a general template. Please have it reviewed against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable medical record regulations before publishing, and insert your grievance officer's contact details.

Information we collect

When you contact us through this website or by telephone, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and a brief description of your medical concern. When you attend the hospital, we collect the clinical information necessary to provide your care.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, arrange appointments, deliver clinical care, maintain medical records as required by law, and communicate with you about your treatment. We do not sell your personal information.

Medical records

Your medical records are maintained confidentially in accordance with applicable Indian medical record-keeping requirements and professional obligations of confidentiality. Access is restricted to the clinical and administrative staff involved in your care.

Sharing of information

We may share your information with other healthcare providers involved in your treatment, with insurers or third-party administrators where you have requested a cashless or reimbursement claim, and where disclosure is required by law or by a competent authority.

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive clinical details through the website contact form.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask for corrections where it is inaccurate, and withdraw consent for non-essential communications. Requests relating to medical records are handled in line with applicable regulations.

Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functioning. If analytics or marketing cookies are added in future, this policy will be updated and consent obtained where required.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your information, contact us on 94954 20145 or write to VISDA Max Care Hospital, Unit of Divine Medical Centre Ltd, Wadakanchery, Kerala – 680582.
