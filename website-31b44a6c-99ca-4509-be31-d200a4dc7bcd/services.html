Treatments & Specialities

Comprehensive orthopaedic care for bones, joints, muscles and spine.

Joint Replacement

Restoring movement and independence

When arthritis or injury has worn a joint beyond repair, replacement surgery can relieve pain and restore function.

Robotic Knee Replacement

Minimally invasive, robot-assisted total and partial knee replacement with 3D pre-operative planning for precise implant alignment.

Learn more →

Total Hip Replacement

Primary and revision hip replacement using modern bearing surfaces and muscle-sparing surgical approaches for a quicker return to walking.

Partial Knee Replacement

For arthritis confined to one compartment of the knee, resurfacing only the damaged area preserves healthy bone and ligaments.

Revision Joint Surgery

Assessment and management of painful, loose or worn joint replacements performed elsewhere, including complex reconstructions.

Shoulder Replacement

Treatment for advanced shoulder arthritis and irreparable rotator cuff damage that has not responded to conservative care.

Deformity Correction

Correction of bow-leg, knock-knee and post-traumatic deformities to restore alignment and relieve joint overload.

Sports & Arthroscopy

Keyhole surgery for sports injuries

Arthroscopic techniques allow us to diagnose and repair joint injuries through small incisions, with faster return to activity.

ACL Reconstruction

Ligament reconstruction for knee instability after sports injuries, followed by a structured return-to-sport programme.

Meniscus Repair

Repair or trimming of torn knee cartilage to relieve locking, catching and pain during movement.

Shoulder Arthroscopy

Treatment of rotator cuff tears, recurrent dislocation and impingement through keyhole surgery.

General Orthopaedics

Everyday bone and joint care

Spine Care

Evaluation and management of disc prolapse, sciatica, neck pain and degenerative spine conditions.

Trauma & Fractures

Emergency and planned fixation of simple and complex fractures using modern implants and imaging.

Arthritis Management

Non-surgical care including medication, injections, bracing and activity modification before surgery is considered.

Osteoporosis Care

Assessment and treatment of bone density loss to reduce the risk of fragility fractures.

Physiotherapy & Rehab

Supervised rehabilitation programmes designed around your procedure, goals and daily routine.

Paediatric Orthopaedics

Care for growth-related conditions, limb deformities and childhood fractures.

The information on this page is for general guidance only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Please consult our orthopaedic team for an assessment specific to your condition.
